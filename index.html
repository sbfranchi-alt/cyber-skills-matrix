What is cyber security?

Cyber security is the practice of protecting systems, networks, applications, data and people from unauthorised access, disruption and damage. Its goal is to keep information confidential, accurate (integrity) and available when the business needs it. These three goals are known as the CIA triad.

The work falls into four broad activities, and each CyberCX practice below sits in one or more of them:

  • Govern Set strategy, policy and risk appetite (S&C, GRC, CCET)
  • Prevent Design and build securely, control access, test for weaknesses (IDAM, NIS, CS2, STA, Secure AI, OTCS)
  • Detect Monitor for hostile activity (MSS, OTCS, CI)
  • Respond Contain, investigate and recover from incidents (DFIR)

A cyber consultant does this work for many different clients. The job is to translate technical risk into business decisions, then help the client act on them.

General skills a cyber consultant needs to start

Technical foundations
  • Networking: TCP/IP, DNS, HTTP, TLS, firewalls
  • Operating systems and directory services (Windows, Linux, AD / Entra)
  • Cloud basics in at least one platform
  • Scripting (PowerShell or Python) to automate and analyse
  • Core security concepts: least privilege, defence in depth, encryption, authentication, threat modelling
  • Awareness of common attacks and MITRE ATT&CK
Frameworks and risk thinking
  • Working knowledge of ISO 27001, NIST CSF and, in Australia, the Essential Eight
  • Assessing likelihood and impact, not just technical severity
  • Prioritising fixes by business value and cost
  • Understanding privacy and regulatory obligations
Consulting skills
  • Clear writing: reports, recommendations, executive summaries
  • Explaining technical issues to non-technical people
  • Stakeholder and client management, workshop facilitation
  • Project delivery: scoping, time tracking, working to deadlines
  • Structured problem solving with incomplete information
Professional traits
  • Curiosity and habit of continuous learning: threats and tools change constantly
  • Integrity and discretion with sensitive client data
  • Calm under pressure, especially in incident work
  • Willingness to pass background checks. Some roles require a security clearance
